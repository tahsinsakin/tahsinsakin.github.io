Network numbers (this device)

Carbon figures are estimates using the method on the receipt: assumed 8 km shop trip minus your farm distance, times 0.12 kg CO2e/km, plus 0.35 kg handling. Not a verified life-cycle assessment. We do not call a purchase zero-carbon.

Farm to Fork Strategy

The Farm to Fork Strategy is the European Commission plan for a fairer, healthier, lower-impact food system. It sits inside the European Green Deal. This app uses the same idea in plain words: a short path from farm to table.

Official Commission page COM(2020) 381 final on EUR-Lex

EU Organic Regulation 2018/848

Regulation (EU) 2018/848 is the EU legal framework for organic production and labelling. Only lots marked EU Organic Certified use that label. Local or short-chain is not the same as organic.

EUR-Lex · Regulation (EU) 2018/848 Commission organics legislation page

European Green Deal

The European Green Deal is the EU growth plan toward climate neutrality. Food systems sit inside that plan. Mention here is context, not a partnership claim.
